Then a 50 Ω test port and the coil tank. RX_IN1 8 and RX_IN2 9 are fed from the match/tank as in Fig. 7-1 | final values: VNA |

**Coil interface (calculated, not tuned):**
- A 28 × 16 mm, 3-turn, 0.5 mm trace/0.5 mm space coil gives L ≈ 0.38 µH, so the tank needs about 363 pF for 13.56 MHz; unloaded Q ≈ 128 [CALC]. That Q is too high for ISO 14443 sidebands, so a damping resistor is needed (TI's example uses 680 Ω in a tank for a 0.95 µH antenna).
- The TI antenna values (68 pF series, 82 pF shunt, 680 Ω) do not transfer to 0.38 µH. Starting point: 330 pF C0G shunt + 33 pF trimmer, 100–150 pF series coupling, damping resistor footprint 330–1000 Ω; **all to be set on a VNA**.
- Coil calculation uses a segment-summation approximation (±15 %); the coil must be field-solved or measured.

TRF7970A needs the DC-DC-free 3V3 rail; VIN accepts 2.7–5.5 V [DS].

### 4.2 IR LED driver and photodiode TIA

LED VSMB10940 [DS Vishay Rev. 1.1]: IF 65 mA DC; IFM 130 mA (tp/T 0.5, tp 100 µs); IFSM 500 mA (tp 100 µs); VF 1.1–1.5 V at 20 mA, 1.35 V at 65 mA, 1.8 V at 500 mA; PV 104 mW; Tj 100 °C; RthJA 450 K/W; tr 15 ns; λp 940 nm.

**Driver:** low-side N-MOSFET **AO3400A** (AOS, SOT-23, LCSC C20917, 483,595 in stock, US$0.09 at 1 pc): Vgs(th) 0.65–1.45 V, Qg 6–7 nC, Ciss 630 pF, RθJA 70–100 °C/W [DS]. Gate from a CC1352R digital output (3.3 V) through 100 Ω.

| Quantity | Value [CALC] |
|---|---|
| LED series resistor | 15 Ω (E24 value; 3.3 V rail, VF 1.55 V, Vds ≈ 50 mV) |
| LED peak current | 113 mA (below IFM 130 mA) |
| Carrier pulse | 1.25 MHz square: 400 ns on / 400 ns off |
| Average LED current, PP4-type burst of 40 µs every 61 / 150 / 284 µs | 37 / 15 / 8 mA |
| Average dissipation | 56 / 23 / 12 mW (limit 104 mW) |
| Junction temperature rise | 25 / 10 / 5 K (RthJA 450 K/W) |

Firmware must cap burst length and duty so that the average stays below 104 mW at the highest ambient; the PP4 timing figures are from third-party sources [S, disputed between sources]. RDS(on) numbers were not extracted from the AO3400A datasheet; 0.1 Ω was assumed (11 mV drop) [A].

**Receiver TIA:** photodiode **VEMD10940F** (Vishay [DS]: λp 920 nm, ±75°, area 0.23 mm², CD 1.3 pF at 5 V, tr 100 ns at RL 1 kΩ, Ira 234 µA at 1 mW/cm² 950 nm 5 V) with **OPA356AIDBVR** (TI [DS SBOS212A]: 200 MHz GBW, 2.5–5.5 V, SOT-23-5; LCSC C183100, 3,002 in stock, US$1.39).
- Proposed: Rf = 10 kΩ, Cf = 1.5 pF (stability estimate Cf_min 0.80 pF for Cin = 8 pF [A]); −3 dB bandwidth 10.6 MHz; Rf noise 4.2 nA rms; gain 10 mV/µA.
- Requirement: ≥ 1.25 MHz carrier ± about 10 kHz [S]. The 10.6 MHz bandwidth leaves margin. A 100 ns photodiode rise time is equivalent to about 3.5 MHz [CALC].
- **Unresolved:** sensitivity and range cannot be computed, because the irradiance of a real transmitter at the label is unknown [U]. The detector after the TIA (band-pass at 1.25 MHz, envelope detection, comparator or capture timer) is not specified.

### 4.4 Antenna geometry versus the stackup

- **2.4 GHz IFA:** TI AN043 (SWRA117D) states that the antenna was designed for a specific USB-dongle PCB, that "small changes of the antenna dimensions may have large impact", that the same PCB thickness and material should be used, and that the board information is in a separate readme **which I do not have** [U]. Therefore AN043 is **not confirmed** for the JLC04161H stackup, and it was not copied blindly: the footprint is a placeholder until a tuned geometry exists.
- **Stackup interaction:** the antenna region has no copper on any layer except the antenna on L1, so the dielectric under it is the full ≈ 1.6 mm (two 0.21 mm prepregs plus the 1.065 mm core, εr 4.4–4.6 per the stackup file). The feed line outside the keepout is 50 Ω CPWG on L1 over L2 (0.36 mm, solved at 50.3 Ω in REV B work).
- **Provisions for tuning:** series-shunt-shunt π network of three 0402 footprints at the feed; a trim-stub pad at the open end for shortening/lengthening; U.FL for conducted S11 with a 0 Ω select resistor.
- **Expected VNA result [A]:** before tuning, a resonance shifted by tens of MHz from 2.44 GHz. AN043 says plastic encapsulation lowers the resonance and the antenna should be made slightly shorter. AN043's design target is return loss better than 10 dB (VSWR < 2) across the 2.4 GHz band; its bandwidth is about 250 MHz in free space and about 100 MHz with a large ground attached. Our ground plane (80 × ≈ 44 mm) is in between.
- **Sub-GHz:** chip antenna (Johanson 0868AT43A0020, 7 × 2 mm, −1.0 dBi peak / −4.0 dBi average [DS]), π footprint; Johanson says the matching values must be re-derived for a customer board. DNP until a sub-GHz target is verified.

## 6. Unresolved protocol and RF compatibility risks

1. **Hanshow and SoluM protocols are proprietary and undocumented.** Sync word, CRC, whitening, data rate, deviation, packet format, addressing and encryption are unknown. The CC1352R has configurable ranges only (`CC1352R_PHY_VERIFICATION.md`); compatibility is unproven.
2. **CC1352R frequency accuracy and synthesiser resolution** are not in the text read; depends on the unchosen 48 MHz crystal.
3. **Dual-band front end:** integrated balun filters out of stock; discrete value list unavailable; sub-GHz front end deferred.
4. **2.4 GHz IFA:** the reference substrate is unknown; resonance must be tuned on a VNA.
5. **ESP32 and CC1352R 2.4 GHz coupling:** 58.8 mm separation gives about 15.6 dB of free-space loss; time division required, simultaneous operation not possible.
6. **NFC coil:** small (28 × 16 mm, about 0.38 µH, unloaded Q ≈ 128 [CALC]); read range and matching unknown.
7. **IR link:** transmitter irradiance, detection method and PP4 timing (disputed between sources) unresolved.
8. **Shield cans:** no verified part; the planned sizes have no matching catalogue frame.
9. **Enclosure:** the Espressif 15 mm housing clearance and the cable passage are enclosure decisions that are not yet defined.
10. **Not production-ready:** nothing has been simulated electromagnetically, routed, or measured.
